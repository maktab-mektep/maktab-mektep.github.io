The school's participants presented their reflections on the possible futures of Aktau, this fascinating city situated between the sea and the desert. Their projects took the form of video works, installations, functional building solutions, and even laboratory experiments, alongside more than ten paper zines—quick sketches and reflections on our experience of being in Aktau.

The final exhibition took place in a temporary exhibition space that we created in the courtyard of Yessenov University's Technopark. During the school, another part of the campus, previously used exclusively as a parking lot, was transformed into a place for gathering, conversation, and collective celebration. The school's closing concert was held here as well.

A total of eight projects were presented by the school's participants. Download the exhibition's brochure to see the details.
