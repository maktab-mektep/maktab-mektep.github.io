Maktab–Mektep is a school that brings research and practice together. We advance ecocritical approaches to architecture in Central Asia through collective action. The school is grounded in the principles of environmental justice, inclusion, and the decentralization of knowledge. This is why we run our programs beyond Almaty, Astana, and Tashkent, focusing on smaller cities where the consequences of environmental crises and social inequality are particularly acute.
